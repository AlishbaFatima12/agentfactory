Claude applies MBA-level business frameworks with the same rigor it applies to code analysis. This lesson covers 10 frameworks organized in three tiers. The four analytical deep-dives -- Harvard Case Study Method (learning through real company dilemmas), Porter's Five Forces (competitive landscape mapping with 1-10 force ratings), Christensen's Disruption Analysis (identifying over/underserved segments and Jobs-to-be-Done), and Financial Statement Literacy (reading income statements, balance sheets, and cash flows as business stories) -- get full worked examples with ready-to-use prompts. The three strategic frameworks -- Marketing Strategy (positioning + channels + 90-day plan), Negotiation (BATNA/ZOPA analysis + tactics), and Pricing Strategy (value metrics + psychology + revenue projection) -- provide structured prompts for specific business decisions. The three leadership frameworks -- Leadership and Management (Stanford's "Touchy Feely" approach), Organizational Design (structure + decision rights + scaling), and Operations (process mapping + bottleneck identification + KPIs) -- address people and process challenges.

The StratArts plugin (`/plugin install stratarts@maigent`) adds 27 structured business strategy skills covering foundation strategy (Business Model Canvas, JTBD, competitive intelligence), market and product (GTM planning, pricing architecture, roadmapping), marketing and growth (AARRR metrics, Traction Bullseye, content strategy), retention and metrics (cohort analysis, NPS, onboarding), and fundraising and operations (financial modeling, unit economics, pitch decks). Plugin skills produce structured outputs -- tables, canvases, scored frameworks -- that are more actionable and repeatable than freeform prompts.

The key insight is framework selection: Porter's Five Forces for current competitive dynamics, disruption analysis for emerging threats, BMC for business model design, TAM/SAM/SOM for realistic market sizing, and financial statements for company health. Real strategists triangulate across multiple frameworks rather than relying on any single lens.
