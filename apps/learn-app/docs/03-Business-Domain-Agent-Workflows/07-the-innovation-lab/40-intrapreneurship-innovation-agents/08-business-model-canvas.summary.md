# Lesson 8 Summary: Business Model Canvas

## What You Learned

The Business Model Canvas maps nine building blocks of any business: Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key Activities, Key Partnerships, and Cost Structure. The critical skill is not just filling in the blocks — it is rating the evidence quality behind each one (ASSUMED, ANECDOTAL, or VALIDATED) and producing a health summary that reveals where execution risk is concentrated.

You also learned to stress-test the canvas with adversarial scenarios targeting the most hypothetical blocks, and to explore alternative business model configurations before committing to a primary model.

## Key Takeaways

- **The canvas is a hypothesis map, not a business plan.** Every block is an assumption until customers validate it — and the evidence quality rating is as important as the block content.
- **The health summary is the most valuable output.** It converts nine isolated blocks into a prioritised risk register: validated blocks are assets; hypothetical blocks are liabilities.
- **Stress-test the hypothetical blocks, not the validated ones.** The risks you have not planned for are the ones that matter most.
- **Alternative business model exploration clarifies trade-offs.** Comparing three alternative configurations forces you to articulate why you chose the primary model — and what it depends on being true.
- **The canvas should be versioned.** A Version 1.2 (post-MVP) canvas is a living document; a static canvas from Month 1 that has not been updated is a liability.
