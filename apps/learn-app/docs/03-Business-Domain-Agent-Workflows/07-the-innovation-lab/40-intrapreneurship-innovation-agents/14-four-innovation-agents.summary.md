# Lesson 14 Summary: Four Innovation Agents

## What you learned

The 10 plugin commands you have used throughout this chapter are reactive; you invoke them when you need them. The four innovation agents are proactive; they run continuously, monitor your venture's key indicators, and surface the decisions that need attention before you ask. This is the shift from reactive intelligence to persistent intelligence.

## Key takeaways

- **Skills are tools; agents are team members.** A skill responds once when you ask. An agent has a standing brief ; it runs weekly, monitors changes, and surfaces patterns you might miss if you only looked when prompted.
- **The Monday Innovation Brief's Uncomfortable Question is its most valuable element.** The unsolicited ideas are useful. The market signal is informative. But the question that challenges a current assumption: the one the team is avoiding: is what makes the brief worth reading every week.
- **The Customer Signal Digest surfaces patterns before they become problems.** Two pilot customers mentioning the same friction in the same week is a signal; three in a row is a pattern that demands action. The digest makes this visible on Monday, not at month-end.
- **The Business Model Architect is trigger-based.** It does not wait for the monthly review. When new validation data arrives, it proposes canvas updates with evidence. A canvas that is not updated when new data arrives is a canvas that is lying.
- **Fundraising readiness has a build time of 3–6 months.** Teams that maintain readiness continuously can move quickly when a warm introduction arrives. Teams that prepare only when they need money scramble , and momentum lost in fundraising is very hard to restart.
