# Lesson 10 Summary: Competitive Intelligence and Market Sizing

## What You Learned

Competitive intelligence and market sizing answer two questions investors ask in the first five minutes: how big is this market, and who else is competing for it? You learned the five-step bottom-up market sizing method (count, qualify, price, calculate, validate) and why it produces more credible results than top-down analyst estimates. You also learned to profile competitors honestly — including their genuine strengths — and to assess the six types of competitive moat.

## Key Takeaways

- **Bottom-up beats top-down.** Counting actual organisations and pricing the opportunity from real customer data is more credible than multiplying an analyst TAM by an assumed market share percentage.
- **The most dangerous competitor is often the incumbent alternative.** For most mid-market companies, the current solution is Excel and WhatsApp used informally. Understanding why customers do not switch tells you exactly what your value proposition must overcome.
- **Every competitor profile must include a genuine strength.** If you only describe weaknesses, investors will not believe the analysis — they know your competitors.
- **The value capture ratio validates pricing.** If your price captures more than 10% of the value you deliver, customers will eventually find alternatives. The validation forces you to either justify the price or revise it.
- **Claim only the moats you currently have evidence for.** Describe moats you are building with a timeline — do not claim a moat that requires 100+ customers when you have 3.
