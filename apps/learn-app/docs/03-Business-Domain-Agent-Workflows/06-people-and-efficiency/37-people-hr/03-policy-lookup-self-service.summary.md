# Policy Lookup — Self-Service Policy Synthesis — Summary

## Key Concepts

- **Policy synthesis**: Transforming legal, jargon-heavy HR policy documents into plain-language summaries that employees can understand and act on without contacting HR.
- **Source citation**: Every policy summary must reference the specific document, section, and date it was derived from — unsourced summaries create false certainty.
- **Verification framework**: AI-generated policy summaries must be checked against source policies for accuracy (correct figures), completeness (all entitlements covered), and currency (statutory rates up to date).
- **FAQ knowledge base**: A structured set of question-answer pairs — each with plain-language answer, source citation, and escalation contact — that powers employee self-service.

## Skills Practised

- `/policy-lookup`: Synthesises complex HR policies into structured, plain-language summaries with quick answer, details, exceptions, contacts, and source citations.

## Key Takeaway

The problem is not that policies are missing — it is that employees cannot find or understand them. `/policy-lookup` translates policy language into plain-language summaries, but the human skill is verification: ensuring every figure, entitlement, and process step in the summary matches the authoritative source policy.
