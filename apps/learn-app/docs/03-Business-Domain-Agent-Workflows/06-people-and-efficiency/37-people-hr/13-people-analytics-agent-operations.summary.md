# People Analytics & Agent Operations: Summary

## Key Concepts

- **People analytics report types**: Headcount (who do we have?), Attrition (are we keeping people?), Diversity (is the pipeline representative?), Org Health (is the structure working?). Each answers a different strategic question.
- **Recruiting pipeline velocity**: Time-in-stage is the diagnostic metric, not pipeline size. Which stage is longest tells you where the process is broken.
- **Agents as sensors**: The four persistent agents (KB agent, onboarding orchestrator, policy maintenance, offboarding knowledge) produce weekly and monthly reports. Those reports are operational intelligence, not system logs.
- **Cross-agent signals**: Patterns that only become visible when reading all four agent reports together, e.g., a KB query spike and a policy inconsistency both pointing to the same document.
- **HR intelligence dashboard**: A small set (6-8) of metrics with defined thresholds and owners. Directs attention; does not replace judgment.

## Skills Practised

- `/people-report`: Generate headcount and attrition analysis; surface the most significant finding and recommend action
- `/recruiting-pipeline`: Track pipeline stages; diagnose friction via time-in-stage analysis; identify the bottleneck

## Key Takeaway

The agents are not just automation; they are sensors. Their reports contain operational intelligence about where the HR function is working and where it is failing. A CHRO who reads all four reports weekly, looking for cross-agent signals, has a continuous early-warning system. One who reviews them separately as system logs has data without intelligence.
