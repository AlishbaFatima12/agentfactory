# Offboarding and Knowledge Transfer: Summary

## Key Concepts

- **Four offboarding principles**: Protect the organisation / Preserve institutional knowledge / Positive experience / Support the team, most processes only deliver Principle 1
- **Four-phase timeline**: Immediate actions (24h) → Handover planning (complete by Week 2) → Exit interview (Week 3) → Last day checklist
- **Handover plan**: Owned by departing employee, reviewed by manager, covers current status + recurring responsibilities + key relationships + open issues + "What I wish I'd known"
- **Exit interview design**: Week 3 timing, HRBP (never line manager), three-section structure (departure / experience / organisational learning)
- **Offboarding-knowledge-agent**: HRIS-triggered automation that runs risk assessment, generates capture plans, schedules sessions, structures notes, and produces a completion report

## Skills Practised

- `/offboard` (hr-operations custom plugin): Four-phase offboarding plan generation with handover table, exit interview schedule, and last-day checklist
- `offboarding-knowledge-agent` (hr-operations plugin): HRIS-triggered automation for knowledge capture coordination

## Key Takeaway

Good offboarding is not a last-day checklist; it is a four-week process that starts within 24 hours of resignation. By the last day, the handover should be complete, the exit interview done, and the knowledge capture programme underway. Everything that matters happens in Weeks 1–3, not on the final day.
