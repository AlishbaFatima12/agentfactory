# Capturing Institutional Knowledge: Summary

## Key Concepts

- **Tacit vs explicit knowledge**: Explicit knowledge is written and findable; tacit knowledge lives in people's heads and leaves when they do. This is what knowledge capture targets
- **Five-factor risk scoring**: Tenure + role criticality + documentation level + successor readiness + client/revenue impact = total score (5–7 LOW, 8–10 MEDIUM, 11–15 HIGH)
- **Proactive vs reactive capture**: Proactive (annual reviews for high-risk holders) produces better outcomes than reactive (scrambling when someone resigns)
- **Three-session interview structure**: Session 1 (client/stakeholder relationships) → Session 2 (methodology and process) → Session 3 (institutional context)
- **Knowledge article format**: Title, the knowledge, when it applies, exceptions, related contacts, confidence level (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW), status DRAFT until reviewed

## Skills Practised

- `/knowledge` (hr-operations custom plugin): Risk assessment, capture plan generation, interview guide, and knowledge article structuring

## Key Takeaway

Most organisations do not have a knowledge transfer problem; they have a knowledge loss problem that becomes visible only after the person leaves. The solution is proactive capture: scheduling annual knowledge review sessions for high-risk holders before they ever think about leaving, while they are fully engaged and their knowledge is current.
