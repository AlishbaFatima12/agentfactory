# Performance Reviews Without Bureaucracy: Summary

## Key Concepts

- **Four Quality Standards**: Strengths (specific and evidenced), development areas (behavioural not personality), maximum 2 development areas, career development (honest and conditionally specific)
- **Vague-to-Specific Conversion**: Observation (observable behaviour) → Why it matters at this role level → Development suggestion (specific action, timeline): the core rewriting discipline
- **Behavioural vs Personality Feedback**: Behavioural describes what happened (disputable with evidence, actionable). Personality describes who the person is (legally risky, not actionable). Every development area must be behavioural.
- **Three Review Modes**: `self-assessment` (before manager writes), `manager [name]` (main review writing), `calibration` (pre-calibration session prep), used in sequence for a complete review cycle
- **360° Synthesis Rules**: Flag anonymity risk for pools < 5, weight by relationship type, identify frequency not just themes, distinguish direction (praise vs concern vs mixed)

## Skills Practised

- `/performance-review` (official human-resources plugin): Three modes, self-assessment template, manager review with vague-to-specific conversion, and calibration prep document

## Key Takeaway

The performance review framework reduces the time managers spend writing reviews without reducing quality. The administrative burden, staring at a blank template, goes to near zero. The feedback quality, specificity, behavioural framing, honest career development, goes up, because the skill provides the structure that most managers find hardest to supply from scratch.
