# Summary: Vendor Management — The Portfolio View

Vendor management fails not from a lack of vendor relationships but from portfolio invisibility. Most organisations have no single, current view of all vendors, costs, contract dates, and usage status. The result is predictable: contracts auto-renew without negotiation, overlapping tools go unrationalised, unused subscriptions persist, and underperforming vendors escape accountability because nobody has structured evidence.

This lesson introduces the `/vendor-review` command as the tool for making the invisible visible. A portfolio audit takes a complete vendor list and produces four outputs: spend by category, immediate attention items (upcoming renewals and unknown-usage vendors), rationalisation opportunities where overlapping capabilities can be consolidated, and a quarterly renewal calendar that creates the negotiation runway needed to act before auto-renewal deadlines pass.

The lesson emphasises that audit output is a starting point, not a final recommendation. Students learn to evaluate the AI's analysis against organisational knowledge — checking whether category assignments are accurate, whether rationalisation savings account for switching costs, and whether renewal dates are correct relative to contractual notice periods. A "What to evaluate" framework teaches the critical judgment that distinguishes a useful audit from a misleading one.

SLA scorecards extend the audit from cost visibility to performance visibility. By comparing contracted SLAs against actual vendor performance data, scorecards produce structured evidence that transforms renewal negotiations from requests into evidence-backed discussions.

The exercise asks students to run a full 47-vendor portfolio audit, evaluate the output for completeness and accuracy, build a quarterly renewal calendar, and identify the three highest-impact immediate actions. This vendor register and renewal calendar carry forward into Lesson 4 (contract analysis), Lesson 7 (compliance), and Lesson 12 (persistent agents).

**Key concepts:** invisible vendor portfolio, rationalisation opportunity, renewal calendar, SLA scorecard, data completeness, addressable vs. realised savings.
