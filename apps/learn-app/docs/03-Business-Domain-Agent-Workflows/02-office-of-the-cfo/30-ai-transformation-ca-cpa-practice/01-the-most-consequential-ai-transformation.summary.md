### Core Concept

CA/CPA practice faces the highest AI transformation stakes of any profession because it combines three structural factors: massive document processing volume, strict regulatory compliance requirements, and fiduciary trust obligations that demand professional judgment.

### Key Mental Models

- **Gen-AI vs Agentic AI**: Gen-AI assists professionals with drafting and research (human stays in the loop); Agentic AI executes multi-step workflows autonomously with minimal supervision -- the distinction determines how much professional oversight each task requires.
- **Five-Domain Impact Map**: CA/CPA work spans five distinct domains (Accounting & Financial Reporting, Tax & Advisory, Assurance, Management Accounting, GRC), each with different AI readiness levels and different boundaries between automatable execution and irreplaceable judgment.

### Critical Patterns

- Routine compliance tasks (data entry, standard computations, document drafting) are most vulnerable to AI automation; advisory and judgment-intensive tasks are most resilient.
- The shift is not "AI replaces accountants" but "accountants who use AI replace those who don't" -- the profession restructures around human-AI collaboration boundaries.
- Each of the five domains has a unique ratio of automatable work to judgment work, requiring domain-specific AI strategies rather than one-size-fits-all adoption.

### Common Mistakes

- Treating Gen-AI and Agentic AI as interchangeable -- they require fundamentally different oversight models and professional responsibility frameworks.
- Assuming AI transformation is uniform across all CA/CPA domains -- tax compliance and audit assurance have very different automation profiles and regulatory constraints.
