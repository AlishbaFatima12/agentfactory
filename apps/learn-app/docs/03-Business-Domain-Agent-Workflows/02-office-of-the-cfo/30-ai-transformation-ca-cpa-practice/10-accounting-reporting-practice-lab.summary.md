### Core Concept

Four hands-on exercises apply the complete plugin and extension stack to real accounting workflows: autonomous bookkeeping from source documents, IFRS financial statements with a deliberate suspense item to investigate, scheduled month-end close with exception handling, and multi-entity consolidation with intercompany elimination -- each exercise reveals where the agent executes reliably and where professional judgment is irreplaceable.

### Key Mental Models

- **Judgment Boundary as Central Learning**: The purpose of each exercise is not producing the deliverable itself but identifying the boundary between what the agent handles (transaction coding, computation, formatting) and what requires the CA/CPA (classifying ambiguous transactions, investigating suspense items, evaluating exception conditions, verifying unrealised profit eliminations).
- **Exception Quality over Happy-Path Quality**: A scheduled task's quality is measured by its exception handling, not its routine output -- an automated month-end close that never asks questions is one that is processing errors silently.

### Critical Patterns

- A bookkeeping agent that never flags transactions for review is coding ambiguous items without alerting anyone -- worse than asking too many questions.
- The trial balance in Exercise 9 deliberately does not balance, teaching students to direct the agent through suspense investigation before producing financial statements.
- The unrealised profit elimination in consolidation (Exercise 11) is the step most frequently done incorrectly because it requires understanding the IFRS 10 principle rather than applying a mechanical formula.

### Common Mistakes

- Skipping the quality review steps at the end of each exercise -- these build the habit of directing verification rather than assuming correctness, which is the professional's core contribution.
- Assuming a scheduled task that runs without exceptions is working well -- silence may mean the thresholds are too high and genuine errors are passing through undetected.
