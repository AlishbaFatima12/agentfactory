### Core Concept

Accounting and Financial Reporting is where AI transforms the highest-volume, most repetitive CA/CPA work -- from journal entries and reconciliations to financial statement preparation -- but professional judgment on recognition, measurement, and disclosure decisions remains firmly human.

### Key Mental Models

- **Gen-AI Assistance vs Agentic Autonomy**: Gen-AI drafts financial statements and researches standards; Agentic AI autonomously records transactions, performs reconciliations, and triggers month-end close sequences -- the boundary sits at judgment-intensive decisions like impairment assessments.
- **IFRS vs US GAAP AI Implications**: Principles-based standards (IFRS) require more professional judgment in AI outputs; rules-based standards (US GAAP) enable more deterministic automation -- the accounting framework directly shapes how much autonomy an AI agent can safely exercise.

### Critical Patterns

- Transaction recording and bank reconciliation are the first processes to reach full autonomy because they follow deterministic matching rules with clear exception escalation paths.
- Financial statement drafting shifts from human composition to AI-generated drafts with human review, collapsing multi-day processes into hours while preserving professional sign-off.
- Accounting research across standards databases (IFRS, US GAAP, local GAAP) becomes near-instantaneous with Gen-AI, but the professional must validate applicability to specific fact patterns.

### Common Mistakes

- Letting AI agents make recognition or measurement judgments without human review -- these require professional expertise that carries legal liability.
- Treating IFRS and US GAAP automation identically -- principles-based standards require significantly more human oversight in the AI workflow.
