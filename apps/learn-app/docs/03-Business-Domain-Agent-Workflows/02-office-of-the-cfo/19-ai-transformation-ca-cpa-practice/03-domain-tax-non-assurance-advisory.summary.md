### Core Concept

Tax and Non-Assurance Advisory splits into two distinct AI trajectories: tax compliance (highly automatable, rule-driven computations) and advisory services (judgment-intensive strategy work where AI augments but cannot replace the professional's synthesis across legal, financial, and business dimensions).

### Key Mental Models

- **Compliance vs Advisory Bifurcation**: Compliance follows deterministic rules (tax codes, filing deadlines, computation formulas) making it ripe for full automation; advisory requires synthesizing ambiguous facts across multiple domains, keeping the professional essential.
- **Due Diligence as AI Accelerator**: Financial due diligence in M&A involves analyzing thousands of documents against checklists -- AI compresses weeks into days by handling extraction and pattern-matching while professionals focus on red-flag interpretation and deal judgment.

### Critical Patterns

- Tax computation agents can autonomously apply tax legislation to financial data, compute liabilities, and prepare returns -- but jurisdiction-specific rules require carefully encoded extensions rather than relying on the AI's general knowledge.
- Restructuring and scenario modelling benefit enormously from AI's ability to run hundreds of permutations simultaneously, but the professional must define the scenarios and interpret results within business context.
- Real platforms (Thomson Reuters CoCounsel, PwC Agent OS, Intuit Assist) already demonstrate production-grade tax AI, establishing that this is deployment-ready rather than theoretical.

### Common Mistakes

- Trusting AI-computed tax figures without verifying against the specific jurisdiction's legislation -- tax codes vary dramatically between jurisdictions and change frequently.
- Conflating tax compliance automation with advisory automation -- compliance can reach near-full autonomy while advisory remains fundamentally human-directed.
