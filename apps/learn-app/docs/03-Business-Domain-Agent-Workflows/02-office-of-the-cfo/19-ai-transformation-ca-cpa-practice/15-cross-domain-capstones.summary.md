### Core Concept

Two capstone exercises integrate all five CA/CPA practice domains: Exercise 22 tests breadth through a full new client onboarding (document intake, risk assessment, analytical review, engagement scoping, SKILL.md extension creation, and client meeting preparation across all five domains), while Exercise 23 tests depth through a three-session annual audit cycle from planning through fieldwork to the audit opinion -- the most legally significant document a CA/CPA signs.

### Key Mental Models

- **Breadth vs Depth Integration**: Exercise 22 connects every domain in a single workflow (the onboarding touches accounting, tax, assurance, management accounting, and GRC simultaneously); Exercise 23 follows one domain (assurance) through its complete lifecycle, demonstrating that real professional work requires both cross-domain awareness and deep single-domain expertise.
- **Audit Opinion as Professional Liability Boundary**: The AI agent compiles misstatements, calculates aggregate effect, and drafts the opinion language in ISA 700 format -- but the decision about whether the financial statements are "true and fair" and the professional liability attached to signing that opinion belong exclusively to the CA/CPA.

### Critical Patterns

- Building SKILL.md extensions during new client onboarding converts a one-time information-gathering exercise into a reusable practice asset -- every subsequent engagement for that client benefits from the encoded knowledge.
- The three-session audit structure (planning, fieldwork, completion) mirrors real practice where these phases are separated by days or weeks, teaching students to save state, produce self-contained deliverables at each stage, and resume with clear handoff points.
- The professional judgment boundary threads through both exercises: in Exercise 22 it is engagement acceptance (deciding whether to take the client); in Exercise 23 it is the audit opinion (deciding whether the accounts are free from material misstatement).

### Common Mistakes

- Skipping the SKILL.md extension step in Exercise 22, treating it as optional -- this step converts one-time engagement setup into a permanent practice asset that improves with each subsequent engagement.
- Assuming the AI writes the audit opinion -- the AI drafts ISA 700-formatted language, but the professional judgment about whether misstatements are material to the financial statements is the CA/CPA's responsibility and the basis of professional liability.
