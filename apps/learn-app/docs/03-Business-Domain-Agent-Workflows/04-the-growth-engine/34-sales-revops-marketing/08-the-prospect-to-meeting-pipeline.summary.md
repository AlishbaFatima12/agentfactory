# The Prospect-to-Meeting Pipeline; Summary

## Core Concept

The six sales skills taught in Lessons 1-7 (research, score, outreach, sequence, brief, follow-up) connect into a single end-to-end pipeline where each stage's output feeds the next. Running the pipeline on a fresh prospect reveals that config quality: especially ICP calibration: amplifies through every downstream stage: a strong ICP produces specific, actionable output at every step, while a weak ICP produces generic output that degrades further at each stage.

## Key Mental Models

- **Pipeline as connected execution**: not six isolated commands but a data chain where research feeds scoring, scoring feeds outreach, outreach feeds sequencing, and so on
- **Config quality amplification (GIGO)**: the ICP is the foundation; a "sell to everyone" ICP inflates scores, strips personalisation from outreach, and empties the pre-call brief of useful intelligence
- **Pipeline quality scorecard**: rate each stage output as READY, NEEDS EDITING, or REDO to diagnose where configuration or prompting is weakest
- **Time-value comparison**: the AI pipeline (~10 minutes) delivers Farah's research depth in Ahmed's timeframe, but only when the ICP is calibrated

## Critical Patterns

- Research is ICP-independent; it produces the same output regardless of config. Amplification starts at Stage 2 (scoring) and compounds through every subsequent stage.
- The weak-ICP experiment shows that a score of 95/100 can be meaningless: if nothing is filtered, everything matches and signal is destroyed.
- Always restore your validated config after running comparison experiments; every subsequent command uses whatever config is active.

## Common Mistakes

- Running pipeline stages in isolation without tracing data flow between them: missing where intelligence is lost
- Assuming a high lead score means a good prospect without examining what the scoring dimensions actually filtered
- Leaving a weak or experimental ICP active after testing, which silently degrades all subsequent pipeline output
- Not auditing later sequence touches for personalisation decay (touches 4-6 often revert to generic templates)
