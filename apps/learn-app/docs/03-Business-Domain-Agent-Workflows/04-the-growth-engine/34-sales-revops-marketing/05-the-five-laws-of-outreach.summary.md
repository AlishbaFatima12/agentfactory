# The Five Laws of Outreach; Summary

## Core Concept

Five non-negotiable constraints separate 35% reply rates from 2%. These laws are not style guidelines: they are hard audit rules that the outreach skill enforces on every message. The lesson also surfaces the Compliance Gap error type: a message can pass all five content laws and still be wrong to send if it is culturally or legally mismatched for the recipient's market.

## Key Mental Models

- **Law 1; Specific Verifiable Reference**: Can the prospect verify this reference in under 30 seconds? If it could apply to any company in their industry, it fails
- **Law 2; Lead with Prospect**: The first substantive sentence must be about their business, not your product. Inbox scanning takes 3-7 seconds; a sentence about them triggers "someone who understands my business"
- **Law 3; One Ask**: Every additional ask reduces the probability of response. One clear, binary question gives the prospect one decision
- **Law 4; Hard Word Limits**: Cold email 150 words, warm email 300, LinkedIn DM 100, WhatsApp B2B 75, follow-up 100. Word limits are a proxy for respecting the prospect's time
- **Law 5; Zero Jargon (17 banned words)**: Synergy, leverage, paradigm, holistic, scalable, best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge, next-generation, disruptive, innovative, groundbreaking, seamless, robust, ecosystem, unlock, empower. The grandmother test: read aloud, replace anything a non-technical person would not understand
- **Compliance Gap** (agent error type #3): Content quality passes all laws but cultural/jurisdictional framing is wrong; UK-style outreach for a Karachi prospect, wrong channel choice, missing regulatory requirements
- **Law-by-law audit as diagnostic method**: Evaluate each law independently rather than making a general "good/bad" judgment

## Critical Patterns

- The outreach skill auto-activates and enforces Five Laws, producing a law check table with the output
- Never trust the agent's self-audit alone: run your own law-by-law check on every message
- When a message violates a law, send it back with a specific correction request (e.g., "Message 2 violates Law 3: two asks. Keep the call request, remove the case study offer")
- Always run a content audit AND a compliance audit separately: content quality and cultural appropriateness are two independent dimensions
- The agent defaults to UK/US business communication patterns; outreach to Pakistan, Gulf, or other markets needs manual cultural adjustment

## Common Mistakes

- Opening with your company name or product description instead of the prospect's situation (Law 2 violation)
- Including multiple asks: a call AND a whitepaper AND a conference meeting: in a single message (Law 3 violation)
- Writing 400-word cold emails that signal "I value my message more than your time" (Law 4 violation)
- Assuming a message that passes all five laws is ready to send: the Compliance Gap means cultural and legal appropriateness must be checked separately
- Using banned words like "leverage" or "scalable" because they sound professional: they sound like every other vendor
