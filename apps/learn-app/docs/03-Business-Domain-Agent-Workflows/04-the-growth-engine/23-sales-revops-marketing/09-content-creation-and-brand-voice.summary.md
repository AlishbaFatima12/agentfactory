# Content Creation and Brand Voice — Summary

## Core Concept

Brand voice configuration is to content what ICP is to the sales pipeline — define it once and every downstream asset inherits it. A single cornerstone article, audited for brand consistency and SEO, can be multiplied into 10+ distribution-ready assets across channels. The content engine closes the production capacity gap: one marketer can match the output of a content team by multiplying thinking, not effort.

## Key Mental Models

- **Cornerstone content** — write one great article and extract many assets from it; write ten mediocre articles and you have ten mediocre pieces
- **Content multiplication** — 1 cornerstone piece produces 10 derivative assets (newsletter, carousel, ad copy, CEO posts, subject lines, etc.)
- **Brand voice as config, not editing** — if tone drifts, fix the config, not the article; the article is the symptom, the config is the cause
- **Volume vs precision content** — text one-pagers (from multiplication) serve the pipeline at scale; interactive one-pagers (from `create-an-asset`) serve high-value shortlist deals
- **/brand-review + /seo-audit loop** — two base plugin commands that audit before multiplication so fixes propagate to all derivatives

## Critical Patterns

- Brand voice config has three layers: tone (how you sound), content pillars (what you write about), and persona-specific voice (who you write for)
- Assets closest to the cornerstone format (newsletter, FAQ) pass brand review on first generation; assets furthest from it (ad copy, subject lines, CEO posts) almost always need iteration
- Short-form content loses brand nuance because compression strips context — generate more variants and discard the ones that score below 75
- Fix SEO once at the cornerstone level; all 10 derivatives benefit from the same structural improvements

## Common Mistakes

- Iterating on the article instead of the config when brand voice drifts
- Treating content multiplication as reformatting rather than genuine format adaptation — all 10 assets should not read like the same blog post with different headers
- Skipping the /brand-review loop on short-form assets (ad copy, subject lines) where drift is most severe
- Using an interactive `create-an-asset` one-pager for every prospect instead of reserving it for high-value shortlist deals
