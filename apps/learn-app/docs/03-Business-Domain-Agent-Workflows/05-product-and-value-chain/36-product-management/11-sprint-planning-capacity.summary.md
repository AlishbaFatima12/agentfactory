## Summary: Sprint Planning & Capacity

- **Key concept**: Real sprint capacity is raw availability minus overhead (PTO, on-call, meetings), then reduced to 70-80% to absorb interrupts. Planning to 100% guarantees misses.
- **Command used**: `/sprint-planning` (official product-management plugin)
- **Artifact produced**: A complete sprint plan for InsightFlow Workflow Builder Sprint 1, including a singular sprint goal, per-person capacity table, P0/P1/P2 backlog within 70-80% capacity, risk table, and Definition of Done
- **Quality criteria**: Sprint load must be ≤80% of planning capacity. Sprint goal must be a single sentence stating ONE outcome. Stretch items must be explicitly labelled as do-not-commit.
- **Next lesson**: The sprint plan feeds into Lesson 12, where /stakeholder-update generates three audience-calibrated status updates about Sprint 1's progress for executives, engineering, and customers.
