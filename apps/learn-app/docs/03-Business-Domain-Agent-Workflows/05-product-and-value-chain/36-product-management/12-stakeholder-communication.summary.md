## Summary: Stakeholder Communication

- **Key concept**: The same underlying status data becomes three completely different documents: executive (compressed, outcome-focused), engineering (technical, decision-rich), customer (benefit-language, jargon-free). Audience calibration is not rewriting; it is translation.
- **Command used**: `/stakeholder-update` (official product-management plugin)
- **Artifact produced**: Three audience-calibrated stakeholder updates for InsightFlow Sprint 1: executive (Yellow status, under 200 words), engineering (with ADR-003 reference and dependency chain), and customer-facing version for CS distribution
- **Quality criteria**: Executive version must be under 300 words with no ticket references. Engineering version must include specific blockers and decisions. Customer version must contain no internal jargon or sprint terminology. Every risk must be classified using ROAM (Resolved/Owned/Accepted/Mitigated).
- **Next lesson**: The metrics review in Lesson 13 will analyse InsightFlow's post-Sprint 1 product health data: the outcomes that the stakeholder updates reported are now measured against targets.
