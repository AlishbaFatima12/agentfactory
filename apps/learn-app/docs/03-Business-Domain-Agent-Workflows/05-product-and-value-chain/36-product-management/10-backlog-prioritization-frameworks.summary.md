## Summary: Backlog Prioritization Frameworks

- **Key concept**: Prioritisation frameworks make judgment visible, not objective. RICE scores are only as good as the assumptions behind them: confidence must reflect evidence quality, not optimism. The three mandatory challenges catch what the framework reliably misses: strategic necessity, evidence gaps, and user love.
- **Command(s) used**: `/prioritise` (custom `product-strategy` plugin)
- **Artifact produced**: RICE-scored Q3 backlog for InsightFlow with all scoring assumptions shown, three mandatory challenges applied, and a quarterly priority decision that names P1 (Workflow Builder + Schedule Trigger), P2 (Bulk Export + Scheduled Reports), strategic overrides (SOC 2 compliance), items not being built this quarter, and discovery spikes for mobile app and cohort analysis
- **Quality criteria**: All RICE assumptions made explicit; three mandatory challenges all run; quarterly priority decision includes the Explicitly Not Building list with rationale; low-confidence, high-effort items have discovery spike recommendations
