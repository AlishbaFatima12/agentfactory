## Summary: Competitive Intelligence

- **Key concept**: A competitive brief is only useful if it is honest — a matrix that rates your product Strong everywhere reflects bias, not reality. The evaluative skill this lesson teaches is the honesty check: would a prospect who evaluated both products find this matrix credible?
- **Command(s) used**: `/competitive-brief` (official product-management plugin)
- **Artifact produced**: (1) A feature comparison matrix for InsightFlow vs AutoDash using the Simple rating scale (Strong/Adequate/Weak/Absent). (2) A differentiate/parity framework specifying where InsightFlow should lead vs where it needs to close gaps.
- **Quality criteria**: Each strategic implication must name a specific capability area, state a competitive stance (differentiate or achieve parity), and specify a product decision. "Improve our analytics" is not a strategic implication.
- **Next lesson**: Lesson 6 uses the research synthesis (L04) and competitive positioning (L05) as inputs to `/write-spec` — the feature specification for InsightFlow's automation builder, which becomes the foundational artifact for Lessons 7 and 8.
