## Summary: The PM's Cognitive Load Problem

- **Key concept**: Product managers occupy five simultaneous cognitive roles (researcher, writer, strategist, communicator, decision-maker) that together create a structural document gap: the volume of writing required to PM well exceeds the time available to PM well.
- **Command(s) used**: None: this is a conceptual foundation lesson.
- **Artifact produced**: A personal PM artifact audit: a list of artifacts produced in the last month, mapped to PM roles, with quality gaps identified.
- **Quality criteria**: PM craft is observable, not subjective. Evaluate specs by testability of acceptance criteria, clarity of out-of-scope, and evidence in the problem statement. Evaluate stakeholder updates by audience calibration and risk visibility.
- **Next lesson**: Lesson 2 installs both plugins and configures product.local.md for InsightFlow: the foundation artifact that calibrates every subsequent command to your product context.
