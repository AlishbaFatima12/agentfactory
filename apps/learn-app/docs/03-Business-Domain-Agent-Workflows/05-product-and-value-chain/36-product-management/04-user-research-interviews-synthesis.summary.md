## Summary: User Research; Interviews & Synthesis

- **Key concept**: The research cycle has two stages connected by a handoff: /interview (custom plugin) generates a behaviorally-grounded guide; /synthesize-research (official plugin) turns structured notes into prioritised product insights. The quality of the synthesis depends on the quality of the guide.
- **Command(s) used**: `/interview` (custom product-strategy plugin) + `/synthesize-research` (official product-management plugin)
- **Artifact produced**: (1) A 45-minute interview guide for understanding how analysts handle reporting without database access. (2) A research synthesis with prioritised findings, behavioral vs stated preference distinctions, and a "what NOT to build" section.
- **Quality criteria**: Each synthesis finding must describe observed behavior, not stated preferences. The "what NOT to build" section must be present and specific. Findings from fewer than 5 participants should be labeled as directional signals, not conclusions.
- **Next lesson**: Lesson 5 builds on the research findings to understand the competitive landscape: specifically, how competitors position their workflow automation offerings relative to InsightFlow's planned expansion.
