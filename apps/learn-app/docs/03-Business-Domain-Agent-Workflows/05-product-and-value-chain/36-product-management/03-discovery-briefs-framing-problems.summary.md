## Summary: Discovery Briefs — Framing the Right Problem

- **Key concept**: Feature requests arrive solution-prescriptive ("build a dashboard") — the PM's job is to reframe them as problem-focused briefs before committing resources, using a structured format that forces problem-focus and surfaces unknowns.
- **Command(s) used**: `/brief` (custom product-strategy plugin) — produces Problem Briefs, Discovery Briefs, and Initiative Briefs.
- **Artifact produced**: A problem brief for InsightFlow's enterprise reporting gap, reframing the Head of Sales' "build a dashboard" request into a structured problem statement with evidence, unknowns, and discovery questions.
- **Quality criteria**: No solution proposals in THE PROBLEM section; WHAT WE DO NOT KNOW section must be present and substantive; discovery questions must be specific and name research methods; every brief must end with a next step.
- **Next lesson**: Lesson 4 (User Research — Interviews & Synthesis) uses the discovery questions from the problem brief to create an interview guide with `/interview` and process findings with `/synthesize-research`.
