## Summary: Metrics, OKRs & Product Analytics

- **Key concept**: Product metrics must be structured in a hierarchy — North Star (single value-delivery signal) → L1 health indicators (six lifecycle areas) → L2 diagnostic metrics (investigation-only). OKRs fail when Key Results measure team output instead of user outcomes.
- **Command used**: `/metrics-review` (official product-management plugin)
- **Artifact produced**: Monthly metrics review for InsightFlow's February data — scorecard with all L1 metrics vs. targets, trend analysis of the activation drop and free-tier churn, two areas of concern with likely causes, and three specific recommended actions; plus Q2 OKRs with measurable outcome-based Key Results
- **Quality criteria**: Every metric must show current/prior/change/target/status. Recommended actions must be specific (named method, owner, timeline). OKR Key Results must be outcome-based — the "could you hit this without users benefiting?" test eliminates output KRs.
- **Next lesson**: The metrics review feeds into Lesson 14's retrospective, where you evaluate whether Sprint 1's success metrics were the right ones and whether the team built what was intended — then deploy three persistent PM agents to automate the intelligence and communication patterns you have built across L11-L13.
