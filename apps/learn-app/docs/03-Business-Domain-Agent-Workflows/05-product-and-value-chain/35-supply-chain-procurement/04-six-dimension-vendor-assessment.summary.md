### Core Concept

A single performance metric creates a dangerous illusion of control: a vendor with 97% OTD can simultaneously have zero financial visibility, a lapsed certification, and an auto-renewing contract with a missed notice window. Six dimensions together give you the full picture.

### Key Mental Models

- **Dimensions Are Independent Risks**: Good performance in one dimension does not compensate for failure in another: sanctions exposure, financial distress, and sole-source dependency exist alongside operational scores
- **Failure Risk Determines Depth**: Strategic and Bottleneck vendors get full six-dimension coverage; Commodity vendors get Commercial and Operational only: the tier from Lesson 3 sets the depth
- **Assessment Output Is a Work Plan**: The ranked action list with owners, deadlines, and risk flags is the deliverable: not just analysis

### Critical Patterns

- Run `/vendor-assess type:"configuration-build"` first to set your thresholds before running assessments: the output is only meaningful relative to your configured standards
- For sole-source vendors: zero financial visibility is a critical risk flag even if every other dimension is green: "I don't know" on financial health is itself a risk
- Sanctions screening applies to every vendor in every industry: any sanctions match requires immediate stop and escalation
- The assessment output ranked action list is directly usable as a CPO work plan with owners and deadlines

### Common Mistakes

- Assuming high OTD means low overall risk: operational performance is one of six dimensions
- Treating the assessment output as a final verdict: quality depends entirely on input data quality; gaps in data are flagged, not hidden
- Believing compliance dimensions only apply to regulated industries: sanctions screening and modern slavery obligations apply universally
