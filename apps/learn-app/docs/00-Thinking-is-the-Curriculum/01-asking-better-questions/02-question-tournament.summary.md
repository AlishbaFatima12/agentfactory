### Core Concept
Question quality is a skill you can evaluate and improve, not an innate talent. The tournament format (comparing your questions against a partner's and measuring which ones actually produce useful AI responses) makes the difference between good and great questions viscerally clear.

### Key Mental Models
- **Evaluation Through Comparison**: Judging question quality in isolation is unreliable; side-by-side comparison against AI responses reveals which questions actually work
- **Actionable vs. Generic**: A question's real quality is measured by its output; does the AI response narrow the problem (actionable) or repeat what any prompt would produce (generic)?

### Critical Patterns
- Generate 15 questions independently without AI before any comparison
- Rank your partner's questions with a one-sentence justification for each; the justification forces precise thinking about why one question outperforms another
- Build a comparison table marking each AI response as useful/actionable or generic/filler with explanation
- Compare top 5 questions from each set across both Claude and ChatGPT

### Common Mistakes
- Assuming more questions means better analysis: 3 great questions outperform 15 mediocre ones
- Ranking questions by topic importance rather than diagnostic power; a question about an important topic can still be poorly formed
- Treating the tournament as a competition to win rather than a mirror to learn from
