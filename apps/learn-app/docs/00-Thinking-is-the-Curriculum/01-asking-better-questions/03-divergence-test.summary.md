### Core Concept
When everyone has the same AI tools, the only differentiator is your thinking. The Divergence Test makes originality measurable by giving the entire class identical inputs and comparing outputs; convergence signals AI pass-through, divergence signals genuine thought.

### Key Mental Models
- **Thinking Partner vs. Answer Machine**: AI collaboration exists on a spectrum from passive acceptance to active critical engagement; the reasoning receipt reveals where you fall
- **Divergence as Signal**: When identical tools produce identical outputs, the student who diverges demonstrated independent thinking; the student who converged likely outsourced it

### Critical Patterns
- Write a uniqueness statement (100 words) identifying what in your analysis reflects your own thinking
- Document every prompt, response, and accept/reject/modify decision in a complete reasoning receipt
- Submit both the analysis and the reasoning receipt; the process evidence matters as much as the output

### Common Mistakes
- Confusing longer analysis with better analysis; originality is about depth and novel connections, not word count
- Using AI extensively but accepting everything without modification; high AI use is fine if engagement is critical, not passive
- Writing a uniqueness statement that cannot point to specific original insights in the analysis
