### Core Concept
AI collaboration quality is not about how much you use AI -- it is about the quality of your decisions about what to accept, reject, and modify. The Collaboration Log makes these patterns visible and measurable.

### Key Mental Models
- **Decision Quality Over Usage Quantity**: The value of AI collaboration lives in the justification column, not the accept/reject ratio -- even correct decisions without reasoning indicate passive collaboration
- **Collaboration Maturity Spectrum**: From Passive (accept everything) through Reactive (reject randomly) to Strategic (deliberate decisions) and Expert (seamless integration)

### Critical Patterns
- Maintain a real-time log of every AI interaction with columns: Prompt Sent, AI Response Summary, Decision, Justification, What I Added or Changed
- Complete a project using AI throughout with at least 15 logged interactions
- Analyze your own accept/reject/modify ratio and summarize your collaboration pattern in 150 words

### Common Mistakes
- Treating the log as paperwork rather than a diagnostic tool -- the log reveals collaboration patterns invisible without it
- Thinking that accepting AI output is always wrong -- sometimes accepting is the correct strategic decision when AI's output is genuinely good
- Gaming the log by artificially modifying or rejecting to appear more critical -- the AI Check catches unjustified rejects
