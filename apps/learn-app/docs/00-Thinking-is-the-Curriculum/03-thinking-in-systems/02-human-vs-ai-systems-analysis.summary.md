### Core Concept
Comparing your systems analysis against AI-generated analyses reveals complementary strengths: humans excel at feedback loops and cultural dynamics while AI excels at breadth of categories. A genuinely merged map (with honest attribution) is better than any single source.

### Key Mental Models
- **Complementary Blind Spots**: Human and AI thinking have structurally different weaknesses; humans miss breadth, AI misses depth and circular dynamics
- **Attribution as Honesty Tool**: Labeling every insight by source (H/C/G/S) prevents self-deception about where your thinking actually contributed value
- **Synthesis vs. Combination**: A genuine merge creates novel connections (S insights) that no single source produced; simply listing everything side by side is combination, not synthesis

### Critical Patterns
- Prompt both Claude and ChatGPT with the same scenario to reveal AI-to-AI divergence, not just human-to-AI differences
- Create three columns: "Effects only I found," "Effects only AI found," "Effects we both found"
- Label every insight in the merged map: H (Human), C (Claude), G (ChatGPT), S (Synthesis)
- Evaluate which source category had the most valuable additions; not the most entries

### Common Mistakes
- Confusing "merging" with "copying everything into one document"; a merge requires judgment about what to include and discard
- Claiming AI-generated insights as your own; the attribution system makes this visible
- Assuming AI insights are always correct; some AI-generated effects sound plausible but would not actually occur
