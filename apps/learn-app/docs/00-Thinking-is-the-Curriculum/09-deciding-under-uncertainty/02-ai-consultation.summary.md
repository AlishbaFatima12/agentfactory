### Core Concept
The hardest part of consulting AI under uncertainty is distinguishing between information AI actually has and information it is confidently fabricating. When the scenario is fictional, everything AI presents as fact about the specifics is fabrication; the skill is noticing this.

### Key Mental Models
- **Fabrication Detection**: AI will generate plausible-sounding analysis about made-up situations. Specific numbers, revenue figures, and market details about a fictional scenario are fabrication, not data. Evaluate the reasoning, not the invented details.
- **Trust Calibration**: For each AI response, explicitly decide whether you trust AI's analysis or your own judgment, and document why. This is not all-or-nothing; you can accept the reasoning while rejecting the specifics.

### Critical Patterns
- Maintain a Consultation Log documenting every AI interaction with trust decisions
- Compare original and updated decisions side by side to see whether AI improved or degraded your judgment
- Update proportionally; neither ignore useful AI analysis nor blindly adopt AI recommendations
- Flag specific sentences in AI output that are fabrication disguised as analysis

### Common Mistakes
- Accepting AI analysis of a fictional scenario as factual data
- Completely changing your recommendation after AI consultation without examining whether the change was warranted
- Treating AI consultation as always improving decisions; sometimes it introduces confusion or false confidence
