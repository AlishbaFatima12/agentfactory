### Core Concept
Every solution is built on assumptions you did not know you were making. The assumption autopsy systematically uncovers these hidden assumptions by comparing your own list against AI-identified assumptions, revealing a consistent pattern: humans catch contextual assumptions (cultural, personal) while AI catches structural assumptions (logical, systemic).

### Key Mental Models
- **Four-Category Assumption Map**: (a) found by you only, (b) found by AI only, (c) found by both, (d) found by neither but identified during merge — category (d) is the most valuable because it emerges only from genuine synthesis
- **Complementary Blind Spots**: Humans and AI have structurally different assumption detection patterns — combining both catches more than either alone
- **Listing vs. Evaluating**: Identifying an assumption is step one; assessing its risk level (what breaks if it's wrong?) is the actual analysis

### Critical Patterns
- First, try to find every hidden assumption yourself before using any AI tools
- Feed your solution to both Claude and ChatGPT, asking each: "What assumptions am I making that I have not stated?"
- Categorize each assumption into the four categories (a through d)
- For each assumption, assess: reasonable, risky, or needs to be tested

### Common Mistakes
- Thinking that listing assumptions equals analyzing them — risk assessment (what happens if this is wrong?) is where the value lies
- Feeling embarrassed by assumptions AI found that you missed — this is complementary intelligence, not personal failure
- Assuming all hidden assumptions are equally dangerous — some are low-risk, others would invalidate the entire solution
