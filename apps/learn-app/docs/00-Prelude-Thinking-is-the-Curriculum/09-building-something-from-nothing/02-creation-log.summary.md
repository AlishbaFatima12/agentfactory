### Core Concept

The Creation Log makes invisible collaboration visible by tracking the origin of every idea -- human, AI, or synthesis. It transforms AI collaboration from a black box into a transparent process where you can see exactly who contributed what.

### Key Mental Models

- **Attribution as Mirror**: The three categories (Human/AI/Synthesis) reveal whether you are creating or outsourcing. True synthesis is rare -- if more than 30% of entries are Synthesis, the attribution is probably inaccurate.
- **Direction vs Following**: Leading AI collaboration means directing with specific prompts and evaluating critically. Following means accepting suggestions passively. The log makes this pattern visible.

### Critical Patterns

- Start from Draft 1 (unassisted) and collaborate with both Claude and ChatGPT
- Log every interaction: your prompt, AI response summary, decision (Accept/Reject/Modify/New Idea), attribution, and what you changed
- Document at least 10 interactions to build a meaningful pattern
- Identify the single most valuable contribution from yourself and from AI in a 100-word note

### Common Mistakes

- Treating the Creation Log as bureaucratic paperwork rather than a tool for self-awareness about creative direction
- Over-attributing to Synthesis to avoid admitting AI drove the idea
- Equating more interactions with better collaboration when quality of direction matters more
