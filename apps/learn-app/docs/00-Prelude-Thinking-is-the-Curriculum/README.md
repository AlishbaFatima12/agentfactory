# Part 0: Thinking is the Curriculum

> _10 Chapters. 10 Skills. Every Exercise Requires You to Think Before AI Thinks for You._

This part comes before agent foundations, before programming, before architecture, before deployment. It comes first because everything that follows depends on it.

AI tools can retrieve facts, generate analyses, write code, and produce polished documents. What they cannot do is think. They cannot ask the right question. They cannot detect their own errors. They cannot reason from first principles when no pattern exists. These ten chapters teach the skills that AI amplifies but cannot replace.

> _Part 0 is about how to teach humans to thrive in the future. The rest of the book is how to build the future._

## What You Need

- A web browser
- Access to **claude.ai** and **chatgpt.com** (free tiers work)
- No code. No setup. No IDE. Just your brain and a browser.

## How This Part Works

Every exercise follows the same cycle:

1. **You think first** and produce a deliverable without AI (or with documented AI collaboration)
2. **You submit** your deliverable to AI using an exact prompt provided in the exercise
3. **AI evaluates** your work, scores your thinking across 5 dimensions, and gives specific feedback
4. **You reflect** on the gap between your self-assessment and AI's assessment

Every AI evaluation uses the **Thinking Score Card** — five consistent scores (Independent Thinking, Critical Evaluation, Reasoning Depth, Originality, Self-Awareness) tracked across all 40 exercises. Your growth trajectory from Chapter 1 to Chapter 10 is the most powerful evidence of your development.

## Assessment Philosophy

The deliverable is never the answer. The deliverable is the documented evidence of thinking. Two students can reach the same conclusion — the grade goes to the one who can show how they got there.

## Before You Begin

Complete the [Thinking Baseline](./01-introduction/03-thinking-baseline.md) — a 30-minute ungraded assessment that snapshots your current thinking skills. You will repeat it after Chapter 10 to measure your growth.

## Learning Path

```
Introduction → Thinking Baseline → Chapters 1-10 → Portfolio Assembly → Post-Assessment → Growth Map
```

Each chapter builds on the previous. Skills introduced early (Prediction Lock, Reasoning Receipt, Error Taxonomy) recur throughout. By Chapter 10, you are using every skill from every preceding chapter simultaneously.
