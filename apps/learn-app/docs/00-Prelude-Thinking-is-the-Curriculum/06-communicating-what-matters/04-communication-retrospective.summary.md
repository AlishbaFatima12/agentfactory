### Core Concept
Delivering difficult news while maintaining a relationship is the hardest communication skill -- and it is entirely human. AI can draft the email but cannot sit across from someone, read their reaction, and adjust in real-time.

### Key Mental Models
- **Honesty AND Empathy**: The best difficult conversations are both honest and empathetic -- these are not opposites but complementary skills that must be balanced
- **Self-Perception vs. External Feedback**: The gaps between your self-reflection and peer feedback reveal blind spots in your communication that you cannot see without external input

### Critical Patterns
- Deliver difficult news with only 60 seconds of preparation and no AI -- rely on instinct and principles, not scripts
- Collect peer feedback on five dimensions: empathy, clarity, honesty, relationship preservation, overall effectiveness
- Write a 200-word reflection and compare it against peer feedback to identify blind spots

### Common Mistakes
- Softening the message so much that the core news never gets delivered -- clarity is as important as kindness
- Treating honesty and empathy as opposites when the best communicators demonstrate both simultaneously
- Treating peer feedback as a judgment rather than diagnostic information about communication patterns
