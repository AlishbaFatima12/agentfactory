### Core Concept
Preparation gets you 70% of the way, but the last 30% -- adaptation under surprise -- determines whether communication succeeds or fails. AI can help prepare, but it cannot adapt for you in the room.

### Key Mental Models
- **Preparation vs. Adaptation**: A polished pitch is necessary but insufficient -- the moment an unexpected concern arrives, only human reflexes matter
- **Adaptation Scorecard**: Three independent dimensions of real-time communication quality -- composure, relevance, and whether the stakeholder felt heard

### Critical Patterns
- Prepare a pitch using any AI tools, then deliver it live to a peer playing a stakeholder role
- Adapt in real-time when the stakeholder reveals an unexpected concern (no AI access allowed)
- Document what happened: the concern, your adaptation, what you wish you had said, and peer feedback scores

### Common Mistakes
- Believing more preparation eliminates the need for adaptation -- the exercise deliberately breaks this assumption
- Conflating composure with having the right answer -- sometimes the best adaptation is acknowledging uncertainty
- Treating peer feedback as a grade rather than diagnostic information about communication patterns
