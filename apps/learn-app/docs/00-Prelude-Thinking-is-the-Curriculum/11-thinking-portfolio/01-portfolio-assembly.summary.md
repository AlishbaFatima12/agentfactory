### Core Concept
The Thinking Portfolio assembles documented evidence of cognitive growth from all ten chapters, and the post-assessment repeats the identical baseline scenario to produce a quantified before-and-after comparison of thinking skills.

### Key Mental Models
- **Portfolio as Foundation**: The portfolio is not an assignment to archive -- every skill documented in it (prediction locks, reasoning receipts, cascade maps, reversal triggers) is called upon by name in Parts 1-10
- **Independent Snapshots**: Baseline and post-assessment must be completed independently (no reviewing baseline before post-assessment) for the comparison to measure genuine growth

### Critical Patterns
- Assemble all ten chapter deliverables with their AI evaluations into a single portfolio
- Complete the post-assessment under identical conditions: 30 minutes, no AI, same hospital triage scenario, same five tasks
- Submit both baseline and post-assessment responses to the AI Post-Assessment Check for dimension-by-dimension comparison and a Thinking Growth Summary

### Common Mistakes
- Looking at baseline responses before completing the post-assessment -- this invalidates the independent comparison
- Treating the portfolio as a completed assignment rather than a living foundation for Parts 1-10
- Undervaluing portfolio items where scores were low -- visible improvement trajectory matters more than absolute scores

### Connections
- **Builds on**: All ten chapters' exercises and deliverables, Thinking Baseline (pre-assessment)
- **Leads to**: Growth Map (quantified comparison), transition to Part 1
