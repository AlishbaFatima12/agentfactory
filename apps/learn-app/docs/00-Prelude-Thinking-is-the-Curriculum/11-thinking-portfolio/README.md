# Thinking Portfolio

> _Part 0 is about how to teach humans to thrive in the future. The rest of the book is how to build the future._

After completing all ten chapters, you assemble your Thinking Portfolio -- a documented record of cognitive growth across question formulation, error detection, systems thinking, first principles reasoning, communication, AI collaboration, ethical reasoning, creation, decision-making, and meta-learning. You then repeat the identical baseline assessment to produce a quantified before-and-after comparison of your thinking skills.

## Teaching Aid

## What You Will Do

- Assemble all ten chapter deliverables with their AI evaluations into a single portfolio
- Repeat the hospital triage scenario under identical baseline conditions (30 minutes, no AI)
- Compare baseline and post-assessment scores across all five Score Card dimensions
- Build a Growth Map quantifying your strongest improvement and biggest remaining gap
- Transition to Part 1 with a clear picture of your thinking strengths and development areas
