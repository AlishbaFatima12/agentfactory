### Core Concept

The Decision Memo synthesizes the full ethical reasoning arc -- initial conviction, adversarial challenge, perspective shift, and formal articulation -- through three visible drafts. The grade lives in the evolution between drafts, not the quality of any single one.

### Key Mental Models

- **Evolution Over Polish**: A weak Draft 1 that visibly matures into a strong Draft 3 demonstrates more learning than a polished Draft 1 that barely changes -- intellectual growth is the assessment, not writing quality
- **Steel-Manning**: Honestly representing the strongest counter-arguments (not straw men) proves you understand the dilemma deeply enough to hold your position responsibly

### Critical Patterns

- Write Draft 1 (500-700 words) before any AI review, including your final position, arguments for and against, stakeholder costs, and reversal conditions
- Submit Draft 1 to AI, revise based on feedback (Draft 2), then finalize after reflection (Draft 3)
- Track what changed between each draft and why in the Evolution Tracker
- Compare confidence level from Exercise 1 to Exercise 4

### Common Mistakes

- Polishing Draft 1 before submission when it should be an honest first attempt with flaws included
- Making cosmetic revisions between drafts rather than genuine intellectual evolution
- Writing reversal conditions that could never realistically be triggered, which is evasion rather than honesty
