### Core Concept

Creation is the act of producing something new -- not summarization, reorganization, or polishing. The Blank Page Sprint establishes what your raw thinking produces with zero augmentation, giving you the baseline needed to understand what AI actually adds in later exercises.

### Key Mental Models

- **Baseline Before Augmentation**: You cannot measure what AI contributes unless you first know what you produce alone. Draft 1 is the honest measurement of your independent thinking capacity.
- **Gap Awareness as Skill**: Knowing what you do not know is a higher-order thinking skill than knowing. An honest gap list demonstrates metacognitive depth that a short one reveals you have not achieved.

### Critical Patterns

- Strict 60-minute timer, no AI, no internet, no notes
- Problem diagnosis first (root causes, not symptoms), then three genuinely distinct solutions
- Recommend one approach with explicit reasoning for why it beats the others
- List every unknown needed for implementation, including uncomfortable admissions of ignorance

### Common Mistakes

- Producing three variations of the same idea rather than three genuinely distinct approaches
- Writing a gap list with only 1-2 items, which signals shallow thinking about implementation
- Trying to produce polished work rather than raw, honest thinking under time pressure
