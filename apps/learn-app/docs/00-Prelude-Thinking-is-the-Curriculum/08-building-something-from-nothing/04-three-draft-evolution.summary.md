### Core Concept
Creation is not a single act but a process with visible stages. The Three-Draft Evolution teaches that your best work comes from iterating between independent thinking, AI collaboration, and honest reflection -- not from any one source alone. The grade lives in the evolution, not the product.

### Key Mental Models
- **Process Over Product**: A rough Draft 1 that visibly matures demonstrates more learning than a polished Draft 1 that barely changes. Document what changed and why, not just what the final version looks like.
- **Three Creative Modes**: Passive (AI did the thinking), Active (you directed, AI assisted), Generative (you created, AI refined). Knowing which mode you operated in is as important as the output itself.

### Critical Patterns
- Submit all three drafts side by side: unassisted (Draft 1), AI-collaborated (Draft 2), reflection-refined (Draft 3)
- Complete the Evolution Tracker for each transition: what was added, removed, changed, and the reasoning
- Write a 200-word reflection answering: What did you create that AI could not have? Where does human value live?
- Compare your self-assessment against AI's rating of your creative process

### Common Mistakes
- Making cosmetic revisions (better writing) rather than intellectual evolution (deeper reasoning) between drafts
- Writing vague reflections without citing specific evidence from the three-draft process
- Over-revising Draft 3 based on AI feedback until original insights are diluted into generic improvement
