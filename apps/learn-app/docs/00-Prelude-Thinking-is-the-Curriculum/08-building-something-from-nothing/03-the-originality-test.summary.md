### Core Concept

The Originality Test answers the most important question about any AI-collaborative work: "Did I actually create something, or did I just facilitate AI's creation?" By comparing your solution against AI's independent output, you measure exactly where your genuine creative value lives.

### Key Mental Models

- **Divergence as Value Signal**: Elements of your solution that overlap with AI's independent version were likely AI-driven. Elements that diverge represent your original contribution -- that divergence is where your professional value lives.
- **The Meta-Question**: Asking "Could AI have produced this?" becomes a reusable self-check for all future creative work. Commodity output is what AI can replicate; valuable output is what it cannot.

### Critical Patterns

- Run the test in a fresh AI conversation with no prior context from your collaboration
- Ask AI to solve the same problem independently first, then compare
- Write a 200-300 word Divergence Analysis identifying overlap, divergence, and unique elements
- Accept that some overlap is expected and not a sign of failure

### Common Mistakes

- Running the test in the same conversation used for collaboration, which contaminates AI's "independent" output
- Making your solution deliberately weird to increase divergence -- originality is genuine insight, not manufactured uniqueness
- Interpreting high overlap as total failure when it actually helps identify which parts are yours and which are not
