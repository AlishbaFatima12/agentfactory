### Core Concept
The Thinking Baseline is a 30-minute, no-AI pre-assessment that creates an honest snapshot of five thinking skills before training. It uses a hospital AI triage scenario and will be repeated identically after Chapter 10 to measure growth.

### Key Mental Models
- **Mirror, Not a Test**: The baseline measures where you start, not where you should be. An honest low score is more valuable than an inflated one because it maximizes visible growth.
- **Cross-Skill Snapshot**: Five tasks preview five different chapter skills (questions, error detection, systems thinking, ethical reasoning, decisions under uncertainty), revealing your relative strengths before training.

### Critical Patterns
- Complete all five tasks in 30 minutes (roughly 5-6 minutes each) without AI
- Submit responses to the AI Baseline Check prompt to receive initial Score Card scores
- Save both your written responses and your AI Baseline Score -- you need both for the post-assessment comparison

### Common Mistakes
- Trying to perform well on the baseline instead of answering honestly -- this reduces the visible growth evidence
- Spending too long on one task and rushing others -- the cross-skill snapshot requires adequate time on each
- Forgetting to save baseline materials before starting Chapter 1
