### Core Concept

Understanding your own work is completely different from producing it. The live defence exposes whether you truly thought through your analysis or outsourced it to AI — if you cannot defend your reasoning without AI access, you did not do the thinking.

### Key Mental Models

- **Defence as Truth Test**: The inability to defend a claim under questioning is evidence that the claim was borrowed, not understood — this applies to AI-assisted work and beyond
- **Dual-Angle Testing**: Human questioning (unpredictable, probing intent) combined with AI counter-arguments (systematic, attacking assumptions) tests thinking from angles neither approach covers alone

### Critical Patterns

- Present analysis to a 3-4 person peer panel for 10 minutes with no AI access
- After defence, feed analysis to AI to generate the 3 strongest counter-arguments
- Write a 200-word response to the strongest counter-argument — engage with the specific attack, do not deflect
- Write a 150-word chapter reflection on question formulation growth

### Common Mistakes

- Treating the defence as a test of having the right answer rather than a test of reasoning quality
- Treating AI counter-arguments as attacks to survive rather than opportunities to strengthen thinking
- Writing a reflection that lists what you did rather than what you learned and what you would change
