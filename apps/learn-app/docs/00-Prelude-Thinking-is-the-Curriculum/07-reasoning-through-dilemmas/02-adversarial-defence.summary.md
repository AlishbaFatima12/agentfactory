### Core Concept
Holding an ethical position under systematic attack requires deep understanding, not just opinion. The three-round adversarial format forces you to strengthen your reasoning or honestly revise your position, revealing whether your conviction is grounded in thought or merely in habit.

### Key Mental Models
- **Depth Through Pressure**: Each adversarial round strips away surface-level arguments, forcing progressively deeper reasoning -- if your position survives three rounds, it rests on foundations, not feelings
- **Position Tracking**: Documenting whether your stance held, shifted, or reversed at each round makes intellectual evolution visible and prevents self-deception about what you actually believe

### Critical Patterns
- AI attacks your position; you defend in writing without AI assistance
- Three rounds: each round targets weaknesses exposed in your previous defence
- Document position changes with exact reasoning in the Position Tracker
- Write a 150-word reflection on which counter-argument was hardest to answer

### Common Mistakes
- Using AI to help write defence responses, which defeats the purpose of testing genuine understanding
- Treating position change as failure when it is actually evidence of intellectual honesty and engagement
- Restating the original position louder instead of addressing the specific counter-arguments raised
