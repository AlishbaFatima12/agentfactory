### Core Concept

The Growth Map quantifies cognitive growth by comparing baseline and post-assessment Score Card scores across all five dimensions, supplemented by 40-exercise trajectory averages that reveal genuine skill trends.

### Key Mental Models

- **Quantified Evidence Over Subjective Feeling**: The Growth Map replaces "I think I improved" with concrete numbers showing exactly how much each dimension changed
- **Gap Awareness as Growth**: Identifying your biggest remaining gap is as valuable as celebrating your strongest improvement -- it directs future practice where it matters most

### Critical Patterns

- Fill in baseline vs. post-assessment scores for all five dimensions and calculate change (+/-)
- Calculate 40-exercise averages per dimension from the Score Tracking Table
- Identify strongest improvement and biggest remaining gap explicitly
- Paste AI's Thinking Growth Summary into the template

### Common Mistakes

- Feeling disappointed by modest growth -- any measurable improvement in thinking is significant
- Treating the Growth Map as the end rather than the beginning of applying thinking skills to technical work
- Ignoring the 40-exercise trajectory in favor of only the pre/post comparison -- trajectory data is more reliable
