### Core Concept

Every exercise in Part 0 is built on six assessment layers that make it structurally impossible to succeed by outsourcing thinking to AI. The deliverable is never the answer -- it is the documented evidence of thinking.

### Key Mental Models

- **Layers as a System**: No single layer catches all cheating, but surviving all six simultaneously requires genuine thought. Each layer closes the gaps left by others.
- **AI as Evaluator, Not Thinker**: AI grades your cognitive process (predictions, reasoning receipts, defences) but cannot produce that process for you.
- **Process Over Output**: Two identical conclusions have different value depending on whether the student thought their way there or copied.

### Critical Patterns

- Prediction Lock: commit a position in writing before touching AI, timestamped and sealed
- Reasoning Receipt: document every prompt, response, and accept/reject/modify decision
- Feedback Challenge Protocol: when AI feedback seems wrong, write a counter-argument and submit it back -- this is graded as bonus critical thinking
- Three-tier scaling: AI first-pass (every student), peer review circles (per chapter), instructor spot-check (flagged cases only)

### Common Mistakes

- Treating the six layers as optional checklists rather than structural requirements embedded in every exercise
- Accepting AI feedback uncritically instead of challenging it when it seems inaccurate
- Assuming the solo learner path is inferior to the peer path -- they develop different thinking skills
