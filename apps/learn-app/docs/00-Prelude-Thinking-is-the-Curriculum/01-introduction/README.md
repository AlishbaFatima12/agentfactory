# Introduction

> _The deliverable is never the answer. The deliverable is the documented evidence of thinking._

This section establishes the foundational architecture of Part 0: the six layers of thinking-proof assessment, the AI feedback cycle, the Thinking Score Card system, and the Thinking Baseline that captures your starting point before any training begins.

## Teaching Aid

## What You Will Learn

- Why thinking skills must come before technical skills in an AI-native curriculum
- The six layers that make it structurally impossible to succeed by outsourcing thinking to AI
- How AI checks your thinking in every exercise (the four-step feedback cycle)
- The Feedback Challenge Protocol for when AI feedback seems wrong
- How the ten chapters connect through a forward dependency map
- The five Thinking Score Card dimensions that track your growth across all 40 exercises
- Your honest cognitive baseline before Chapter 1 begins
