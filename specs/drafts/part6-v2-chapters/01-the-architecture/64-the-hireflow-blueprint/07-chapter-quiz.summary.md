### Core Concept

The chapter quiz tests recall and understanding of Domain Decomposition's six steps, the seven blueprint template sections, and the key distinctions introduced in Chapter 64: wish vs. blueprint, type annotation vs. data contract, and gate placement reasoning.

### Key Mental Models

- **Ten questions covering five concept areas**: wish vs. blueprint, step order, boundary failures, data contracts, gate placement, role specification components, economic participation, success criteria, maturity model placement, and blueprint-to-concept-paper relationship.
- **Scoring guide calibrates readiness**: 9-10 means ready for Chapter 65. Below 5 means re-read the full chapter. The guide is explicit rather than leaving the student to self-assess without a benchmark.

### Critical Patterns

- All ten quiz answers connect to the blueprint's practical purpose, not trivia about the James/Emma narrative
- Questions 5 and 7 are the highest-leverage: gate placement reasoning and economic participation rationale are the most commonly misunderstood concepts

### Common Mistakes

- Confusing "deployment region" as a role specification component (it is not; it is an infrastructure concern)
- Believing gates should be placed at every stage rather than at high-leverage decision points
- Treating "the factory should be fast" as a valid success criterion
