# Chapter 63: The Agent Maturity Model

In Chapter 61, you learned what an Agent Factory is and how the Two-Layered Model governs it. In Chapter 62, you explored why factories need budget infrastructure before agents become economic actors. Those chapters answered two questions: _who governs the factory?_ and _what is it becoming?_

This chapter answers the third: _how does a factory grow from idea to production?_

You will learn why jumping straight to "build me an agent" produces brittle, domain-ignorant systems. You will discover the five phases that transform a vague idea into a reliable factory, and you will meet the concept of **crystallization**: the moment when general exploration produces enough clarity to construct a specialist.

## What You Will Learn

- Why asking a general AI to "build a resume screener" produces something that runs but does not work
- The two roles general agents play in factory construction: **Incubator** (Director) and **Builder**
- **Crystallization**: the phase transition from exploration to construction
- The **Five-Phase Map**: Explore, Incubate, Build Specialist, Cloud Deploy, Scale
- The **9.5+ threshold**: the quality signal that triggers crystallization
- Why general agents BUILD custom agents, instead of becoming them

## Prerequisites

- **Chapter 61 complete**: You understand the Agent Factory, the Two-Layered Model, and HireFlow's four Digital FTEs
- **Chapter 62 complete**: You understand why factories need budget infrastructure and economic participation points

## Key Terms Introduced

| Term                      | Definition                                                                                         |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Incubator**             | A general agent acting as Director: exploring the domain, writing concept papers, validating ideas |
| **Specialist**            | A custom agent built for one job, constructed from validated domain knowledge                      |
| **Crystallization**       | The phase transition from fluid exploration to structured construction                             |
| **Five-Phase Map**        | The development journey: Explore, Incubate, Build Specialist, Cloud Deploy, Scale                  |
| **9.5+ threshold**        | The quality score that signals crystallization: domain knowledge is solid enough to build          |
| **Phase transition**      | The boundary between exploration and construction, triggered by the quality threshold              |
| **Domain-ignorant agent** | An agent that executes syntax correctly but lacks domain knowledge to produce useful outputs       |
