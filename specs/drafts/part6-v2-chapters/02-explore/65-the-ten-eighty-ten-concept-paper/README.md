# Chapter 65: The 10-80-10 Concept Paper

In Chapter 64, you produced a Factory Blueprint for HireFlow: four FTE role specifications, data contracts, human review gates, and success criteria. The blueprint says what the factory DOES. But it does not answer a harder question: should this factory EXIST?

This chapter teaches the validation step between architecture and implementation. You will learn the **10-80-10 Rule**: a method for writing a concept paper that combines research, domain knowledge, and AI-assisted validation. By the end, you will have a concept paper for HireFlow and the ability to write one for any factory you design.

## What You Will Learn

- Why a blueprint alone is not enough to start building
- The 10-80-10 Rule: ten percent research, eighty percent domain knowledge, ten percent AI validation
- The Multi-LLM Exploration method: using frontier models as an advisory board
- The 9.5+ quality threshold and when a concept paper is ready to proceed
- How to write the HireFlow concept paper using your Ch 64 blueprint

## Prerequisites

- **Chapter 61 complete**: You understand the Agent Factory and the Two-Layered Model
- **Chapter 63 complete**: You understand the Agent Maturity Model and the five-phase journey (especially Phase 1: Explore)
- **Chapter 64 complete**: You have a Factory Blueprint for HireFlow with role specs, data contracts, and success criteria

## Key Terms Introduced

| Term                       | Definition                                                                                         |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Concept Paper**          | A persuasion document that argues a factory SHOULD exist, backed by research and domain knowledge  |
| **10-80-10 Rule**          | The ratio for concept paper creation: 10% research, 80% domain knowledge, 10% AI validation        |
| **Multi-LLM Exploration**  | Using multiple frontier models to challenge and refine a concept paper from different perspectives |
| **Advisory Board Pattern** | Treating different LLMs as different domain experts who review the same document                   |
| **Quality Threshold**      | The 9.5+ score (out of 10) at which a concept paper has earned the right to proceed to Phase 2     |
| **Crystallization Signal** | The moment when AI feedback shifts from substantive critique to stylistic preference               |
