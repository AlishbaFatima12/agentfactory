### Core Concept

The Chapter 65 quiz assesses understanding of four key concepts: the Blueprint vs. Concept Paper distinction, the 10-80-10 Rule (structure and diagnostic use), the Advisory Board Pattern (three roles, feedback classification), and the crystallization signal. Ten questions, 80% pass threshold.

### Key Mental Models

- **10-80-10 as Diagnostic**: If research needs more than 10%, domain knowledge is insufficient and the builder should return to exploration before writing the concept paper (Q4).
- **Crystallization Signal Test**: A paper at 9.5+ with only stylistic feedback is done. Continuing to iterate past crystallization is procrastination (Q8). The signal is the nature of the feedback, not the score alone.
- **FTE Justification Edge Case**: The Interview Question Generator synthesizes two input streams, making synthesis the distinct expertise. Distinct Input does not require a single source (Q9).

### Critical Patterns

- The concept paper closes Phase 1 (Explore) and sits at the Phase 1/Phase 2 boundary. The Domain Mastery Gate, not the concept paper, is the actual transition point (Q10).
- Three different LLMs are used because different models have different blind spots, not because three models are architecturally different or because one cannot be prompted differently (Q5).
- AI validation is last because the builder needs domain understanding built through research and blueprint work to evaluate AI feedback; without it, they cannot distinguish valid critique from generic advice (Q3).
