### Core Concept

The Domain Mastery Gate is the quality gate between the Explore and Incubate phases of the Agent Maturity Model. Its five steps formalize what James and Emma worked through informally: Generate a 30-question quiz from the concept paper, Take it without AI, Score using dimension-specific rubrics, Remediate using primary sources, Re-test with new questions on gap areas. Without the gate, principals move from the concept paper to skill writing carrying blind spots they cannot see, and those blind spots become permanently encoded in agent behavior.

### Key Mental Models

- **Gate position in the maturity model**: The gate sits between Explore (concept paper) and Incubate (skill writing). Passing it authorizes the transition. Skipping it means simulations look clean because the test cases carry the same blind spots as the skills.
- **Two-instrument measurement**: The concept paper score measures the quality of the document. The mastery quiz score measures the depth of the author. A 9.6 paper score and a 65% quiz score are not contradictory. They measure different things.
- **Blind spot inheritance (formal definition)**: When a principal's unrecognized domain gaps propagate into agent behavior because those gaps are never encoded as constraints, exceptions, or decision rules in the agent's skill definitions. The word "unrecognized" carries the full weight: a known limitation can be handled with an escalation rule. Only unknown gaps produce silent failures.

### Critical Patterns

- Mastery threshold: 85% overall, no single dimension below 70%. Per-dimension floors catch uneven preparation that an overall average would mask.
- Remediation must use primary sources (regulations, textbooks, industry standards) because AI summaries may gloss over the same nuances the student is trying to acquire
- The gate is a recurring check, not a one-time diploma. Domain extensions, new industry verticals, and regulatory changes all trigger a new pass through the gate.

### Common Mistakes

- Skipping the gate because the concept paper scored well (the paper tests document quality; the gate tests the author)
- Using AI assistance during quiz-taking (measures AI capabilities, leaves the principal's blind spots hidden)
- Re-taking the original quiz after remediation instead of generating new questions (tests memory of answers, not genuine understanding)
