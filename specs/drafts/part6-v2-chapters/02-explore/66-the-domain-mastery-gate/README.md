# Chapter 66: The Domain Mastery Gate

## Overview

Before converting domain knowledge into agent skills (Chapter 67), you must verify that your knowledge is deep enough to supervise agents working in that domain. This chapter introduces the Domain Mastery Gate: a quality gate between the Explore and Incubate phases that protects you from building agents on top of blind spots.

## Lessons

1. **James Skips Ahead**: Opening scenario. James tries to jump from concept paper to skill writing. Emma reveals the gaps in his recruitment domain knowledge with five rapid-fire questions.

2. **From Paper to Quiz**: Guided discovery. How to generate a comprehensive mastery quiz from your concept paper across three dimensions (facts, edge cases, judgment calls).

3. **The Mastery Gate Pattern**: Concept crystallization. The five-step formal pattern (Generate, Take, Score, Remediate, Re-test), the mastery threshold, and why agents inherit your blind spots.

4. **Take the HireFlow Domain Quiz**: Applied exercise. Generate your own quiz from your HireFlow concept paper, take it AI-free, score yourself, and create a remediation plan.

5. **Reflection and Chapter Quiz**: Connection to Chapter 67 (skill writing), the Dunning-Kruger defense, and 10 multiple-choice questions.

## Key Concepts

- **Domain Mastery Gate**: quality gate between Explore and Incubate phases
- **Three Quiz Dimensions**: facts, edge cases, judgment calls
- **Mastery Threshold**: 85%+ overall, no dimension below 70%
- **Blind Spot Inheritance**: agents reproduce the principal's domain gaps
- **Remediation Loop**: study primary sources, generate new questions, re-test
