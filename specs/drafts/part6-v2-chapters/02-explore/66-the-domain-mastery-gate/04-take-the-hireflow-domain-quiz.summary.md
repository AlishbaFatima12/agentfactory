### Core Concept

This lesson applies all five steps of the Domain Mastery Gate to the HireFlow domain. James scores 78%: 90% on facts, 65% on edge cases, 70% on judgment calls. He thought a 20-page concept paper meant he understood recruitment. The quiz revealed he understood the happy path. Employment gaps, conflicting candidate signals, and anti-discrimination rules were absent from his mental model because he had never needed to articulate them. Four gaps on his remediation plan represent four places where his agent skills would have made quiet mistakes.

### Key Mental Models

- **The quiz generation prompt**: The 30-question format (10 fact, 10 edge case, 10 judgment call) uses the concept paper as raw material. Weak questions test recall of the paper's wording; strong questions apply domain knowledge to scenarios the paper never addressed.
- **AI-free discipline**: Closing AI and notes is not a formality. If AI answers the quiz, the results map the AI's capabilities rather than the principal's blind spots. The whole point is to expose what the principal does not know.
- **Remediation plan with primary sources**: AI summaries may carry the same gaps the student is trying to fix. Primary sources (regulations, practitioner guides, textbooks) provide independent ground truth that does not depend on what any AI model was trained on.

### Critical Patterns

- Blank answers and honest "I do not know" entries are the most valuable gate output; they locate blind spots precisely
- Re-testing generates new questions on gap areas only; re-taking the original quiz tests memory of answers, not genuine improvement
- Each row in the remediation plan maps one gap to one primary source with a target study date

### Common Mistakes

- Accepting quiz questions that test paper recall rather than domain application
- Skipping the re-test after remediation (fixes the study but never verifies the knowledge transferred)
- Using AI summaries as remediation sources (same circularity problem: the summary may not cover the exact nuance you are missing)
