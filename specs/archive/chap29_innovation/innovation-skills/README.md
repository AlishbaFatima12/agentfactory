## The DLA Stack

This library implements the three-methodology innovation stack:

```
DESIGN THINKING ──► LEAN STARTUP ──► AGILE
  /discovery            /hypothesis      /sprint
  /idea                 /validate        (+ Chapter 25 Product skills)
  /canvas (early)       /canvas (v2)
  
  ↓ all stages:
  /market  /financials  /pitch  /gtm
```

**Design Thinking** (problem level): Understand what is really worth solving
  Commands: /discovery, /idea
  Outputs: JTBD map, pain ranking, HMW problem statements, idea shortlist

**Lean Startup** (solution level): Test whether your solution works
  Commands: /hypothesis, /validate, /canvas
  Outputs: Assumption map, MVP design, build-measure-learn analysis, BMC

**Agile** (delivery level): Build and adapt efficiently
  Commands: /sprint
  Outputs: Sprint plan, backlog, retrospective, assumption updates

## The Governing Principle

> **The goal of innovation is not to have a great idea.
> It is to convert uncertainty into validated opportunity
> faster than the competition.**
>
> AI does not validate your idea for you.
> It removes the execution overhead that kept most good ideas
> from being properly explored — the hours of synthesis,
> structuring, and first drafts that should have been spent
> on thinking, testing, and building.
