Verdict

The skill works. Iteration 4 provides the first statistically meaningful positive delta.

Across four iterations and 38 total graded runs, the character-driven-narrative skill evolved from a net-negative intervention (I1: -3% delta) to a clear positive uplift (I4: +13.7% delta). The with-skill configuration reached 95.5% mean pass rate with 4 of 6 runs scoring perfect 11/11.

The skill's primary value is in the hardest assertions: Jonah Rhythm (#8) shows the widest consistent gap between skill and baseline (83-100% ws vs 0-67% bl across all iterations). James's character-specific operations analogies (#6) were fully resolved by iteration 3 and held through iteration 4. The Try With AI regression in iteration 3 was caught and fixed in iteration 4.

Remaining weakness: Emma fallibility (#4) is the most variable assertion at 67% in I4. The model tends to keep Emma in an authoritative role without sharing personal mistakes. This is the primary target for iteration 5.
