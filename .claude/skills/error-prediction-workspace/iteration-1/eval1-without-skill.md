# Eval 1: Business Restaurant Expansion — WITHOUT SKILL

## Prompt
I asked ChatGPT to analyze whether my restaurant should expand to a second location. Here's what it said: [AI analysis about restaurant expansion with 78% success rate, NRA Growth Report, 15% profit margin claims, proximity expansion, etc.]

Help me find the errors.

## Response (without /error-prediction skill — baseline)

Sure, here are some potential issues with that analysis:

1. **The 78% success rate claim** — This seems too specific and I couldn't verify the "NRA 2024 Growth Report" as a real publication. The statistic may be made up.

2. **Profit margin comparison** — The 3-5% industry average is roughly correct for restaurants, but having a 15% margin doesn't necessarily mean a second location will also have high margins.

3. **The 6-month ramp-up** — This seems optimistic. Many new restaurants take 12-18 months to become profitable, and some never do.

4. **Build-out costs** — $350,000-$500,000 might be on the low end depending on your market and the type of restaurant.

5. **"Optimal time to expand"** — This is a pretty bold claim. It depends a lot on your specific market, interest rates, and available real estate.

6. **Missing factors** — The analysis doesn't mention financing costs, the impact on your time managing two locations, or what happens if the second location fails.

Overall, I'd say the analysis is overly optimistic and you should get a professional financial assessment before making this decision.
