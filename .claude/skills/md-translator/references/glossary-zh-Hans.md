# Glossary: Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans)

## Convention

| Convention | Rule |
|------------|------|
| **[EN]** | Keep in English |
| **[TR]** | Transliterate to Chinese characters (phonetic approximation) |
| **[TL]** | Fully translate to Simplified Chinese |
| **First use** | Use the translated term by default. Add `(English term)` on first occurrence only when clarity, searchability, or repo convention requires it. |

## Notes on Simplified Chinese

- Simplified Chinese is **LTR** — BiDi rules (43-50) do NOT apply
- Use Simplified Chinese characters (简体字), not Traditional (繁體字)
- Locale code: `zh-Hans` (BCP 47) or `zh-CN` (common in web frameworks)
- Technical terms in Mainland China conventions often differ from Taiwan translations
- Prefer Mainland China standard terminology where established
